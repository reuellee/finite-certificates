### Target A: universal mixed chain and specialization theorem

Target A is conditional on a declared lower-skeleton/source interface
`L_source`: labeled lower joined generators, orientations, boundary tags and
specialization maps with an explicit input schema. A may use that interface
but may not assume that the complete complex, comparison or exactness claimed
in B already exists. B must separately construct and verify `L_source` from
the original bad-locus and Gordan-witness data before invoking A. This keeps
the A-to-B dependency acyclic.

For every realizable uniform rank-four parent on eight elements, every
normalized parent realization cell, every proper antichain of pairwise
incomparable proper extension signatures, every ordered active subfamily of
size `r <= 3` modulo the corresponding block permutation, and every admissible
witness-support stratum, require one finite **simultaneous** semialgebraic
subdivision compatible with all active blocks, weights, supports, charts and
boundary strata. On it, construct an integral, genuinely mixed `(1,0,0)`
chain family, equivariant under the three permutations of `(1,0,0)`, whose
relative boundary is the required primitive flow-disk chain
`(-1,1,1,1,1,1,1)` plus explicitly named certified lower joined faces or
genuine true-parent-infinity faces.

The assignment must be natural for support loss, zero block weights, chart
overlap, monodromy, parent-boundary specialization and witness degeneration.
It must commute coherently along **every arbitrary composable face chain**,
not merely across individual codimension-one squares. Parent-matrix rank drop
and witness-matrix/support rank drop are distinct strata: the former may be a
true parent boundary when certified by the actual compactification; the
latter is an internal witness specialization unless a separate theorem proves

Target A makes **no global rank, exactness, pair-kernel, triple-escape or D3
claim**. Even a complete proof of A is feasibility evidence for B, not closure

## Midpoint gate

The midpoint fires when topology, falsifier and naturality have each supplied
their first complete handoff, or immediately when any lane returns an exact
`NEGATIVE` or identifies a decisive `NULL` seam.

At midpoint the coordinator freezes the claim/dependency graph. A second and
final handoff round is allowed only if all of the following hold:

1. A, B and C each has an explicit finite dependency list and no unnamed
   classification theorem or exhaustive computation;
2. A covers the proper-antichain domain, every `r<=3` active subfamily modulo
   permutation, one simultaneous subdivision, every specialization and
   arbitrary face-chain coherence, with no rank claim;
3. B has finite obligations for every missing face type, source provenance,
   filtration-preserving comparison and rational pair-kernel exactness;
4. C has a separate finite route from each actual triple component to certified
   true parent infinity. A's relative-boundary bookkeeping, witness rank drop
   and B's quotient do not satisfy this condition;
5. the falsifier has not produced a correctly scoped universal counterexample,
   and a complete finite `POSITIVE` proof-program or named-level `NEGATIVE`
   remains reachable in the single revision without increasing deliverables.

If any condition fails, discovery stops and the frozen material goes directly
to the referee. A-only success stops as `INFORMATIONAL / STOP`. Midpoint
continuation and a positive proof-program are not evidence that A, B or C is a
proved theorem.
