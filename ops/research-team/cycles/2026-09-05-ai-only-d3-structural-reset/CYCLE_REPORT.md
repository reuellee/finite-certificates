### 4. The complete fixed-plane rank criterion also fails

Three or more incident support planes can share an escape line. The final
bounded test therefore replaced the row-count restriction by the actual
linear rank condition. For each pair of incident normals, it retained every
star normal in their span, together with all 35 nonstar rows. These rank
closures exhaust all possible incident witness supports of rank at most two.
Only the already registered upper chart zero was tested; no new parent
configuration or atlas was added.

The coordinator and a separate falsifier independently excluded every
expanded restriction. There are 896 distinct labeled closures: 56 containing
six star rows and 840 containing two. Counting the original pair indices,
840 choices have size six and 840 have size two. The exact certificates check
65,520 strict inequalities using the same old 23-vector pool. The coordinator's
deterministic replay needs only 11 of those vectors. Both replays reconstruct
the closures exactly; their proof is not an assumption that an old separator
continues to work after adding rows.

This means that for every triple of nonnegative Gordan witnesses at the
point, the incident support normals span dimension exactly three at every
parent label. Their support planes pin the corresponding column ray. This
excludes a nontrivial motion of one column that preserves all those selected
support planes. Motions allowing the planes to rotate remain possible,
including motions with unchanged support-row indices.

The accepted stronger openness proof checks nonzero rank-two pair minors,
28,560 rank-three exclusion minors, strict separator margins, and the three
full positive rank-four circuits. Nearby rank closures can only shrink after
all finitely many nonzero minors are preserved, so the same separators remain
sufficient. The [final independent referee](../../../team/ai-d3-rank-pencil-referee/REVIEW.md)
accepted the complete-rank result, conditional escape argument, and openness
corollary. Its separate integer reconstruction evaluated all 141,120 coordinate
3-by-3 minors and rejected 12 hostile mutations, including a separator that
passes the old restriction but fails an added closure row.

Neither a compact component of the actual triple-bad locus nor a failure of
D3 has been established. The independent pair-Hc1 obligation remains open.
