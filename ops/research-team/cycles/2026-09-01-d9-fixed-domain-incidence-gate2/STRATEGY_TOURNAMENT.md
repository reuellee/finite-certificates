unresolved authorization conditions from commit `32e2b37`: fixed-domain
multiwall compression and specialization of the deterministic projection.

proved to preserve the entire active factor set.  This cycle computes that
stabilizer instead of assuming it.

small producer-independent exact-rational replay.  Passing that screen is
only partial evidence; it cannot authorize the remaining 3,456 higher-degree
systems.

The cycle is terminal for this constructive continuation.  A complete filter
and complete specialization certificate authorize the roadmap census.  An
invalid quotient, non-generic factor, or surviving complete-incidence gap
forces a pivot away from another unfiltered enumeration attempt.
