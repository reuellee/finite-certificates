# Direct-proof source and target audit

Conventional deductive proofs are fully acceptable in this cycle. A finite
orbit census, executable certificate and proof-assistant formalization are
not prerequisites for an original theorem. Computations used as evidence
must, however, be independently checked. This note records source-level
dependencies, not newly earned theorem credit.

## Original scope

ATLAS_HELLY.md defines the third entry for every realizable uniform rank-four
parent on eight labeled elements and every three signatures whose nonempty
proper feasibility regions are pairwise incomparable. Its internal-antichain
quantifier is broader than the inclusion-minimal family E(M) used for atlas
coloring. A counterexample or proof of 9DVL must respect the internal-antichain
scope; it need not first establish global minimality in E(M).

The exact target is H6 of the common good locus, equivalently Hc2 of the
union of the three bad loci. The latter equivalence needs only H7(X)=H6(X)=0,
which follows from the established rank-four/eight-element contraction-height
bound. The stronger blanket contractibility statement and its historical
58-class source-trace issue are unnecessary for this diagonal. This reduction
was already accepted in the previous checkpoint and receives no new credit.

The three-block closed-cover spectral sequence, together with the established
single-bad Hc0,Hc1,Hc2 vanishings and pair-bad Hc0 vanishing, leaves exactly
the original triple Hc0 group and the kernel of the alternating pair Hc1 map.
Both are permanent associated-graded contributions in total degree2. Thus
closing only one is insufficient. This is the existing JOINED_FLOW theorem,
not a new conclusion of this cycle.

The balanced-end description in DIAG3_PAIR_DIFFERENTIAL_ENDS assumes triple
Hc0=0 as well as pair Hc0=0 when presenting its short exact sequence. It is
safe to use it after separately proving triple noncompactness, or to retain
its full derived triangle before then. Under that hypothesis, exclusive-pair
Hc1 vanishing plus balanced-frontier injectivity is equivalent to the pair
target. Individual restriction injectivity or pairwise-disjoint images do
not imply joint three-image independence.

Universal two-factor Hc1 and three-factor Hc0 vanishings are sufficient but
stronger endpoints. The previously proved 8902/9476 stronger pair-wall cases
do not promote the original pair map or original triple target. Neither the
574 remaining pair orbits nor the1162302 triple source records must be
exhausted if a direct original-scope theorem is proved.

The proper full convex Gordan correspondence already compares with each
actual bad locus, including changing witness supports. The nonexistence of
a continuous single-witness selector does not invalidate this proper acyclic
comparison. A new proof may use full correspondences and conventional
topological arguments without constructing a forbidden selector.

## Narrow literature applicability check

A short primary-source search did not identify a theorem with the exact
three-region realization-space quantifier required here. This is not an
exhaustive literature claim.

* Corey and Luber prove smoothness for complex matroid realization spaces
  of rank4 on at most9 elements. Smoothness of those algebraic spaces is
  not vanishing of real compact-support Hc2 for unions of the present bad
  regions. [Singular matroid realization spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.11915).
* Liu's extension-space counterexample concerns the poset space of extensions
  of a realizable oriented matroid of high rank and corank3. It supplies
  neither the present rank4/eight-parent theorem nor its negation. Extension
  poset topology must not be substituted for intersections of feasibility
  loci over varying realizations. [A counterexample to the extension space
  conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.05033).

These checks add no proof dependency to the accepted finite or deductive
results. The direct candidate review remains the substantive work.

## Exact next proof obligations

The feasible-third-vector normalization produces an actual affine
convex-hull-intersection model for the two bad blocks on an exclusive-pair
stratum. A deduction still needs the global low-degree cohomology of that
model and mixed-frontier independence. Forgetting the private vector incurs
a compact-support degree shift; ordinary homotopy equivalence is inadequate.

For triple noncompactness, a local deformation excludes a compact component
only when it supplies an appropriate maximum principle or a proper escape.
A nonzero tangent vector or a one-sided curve is not enough. A nontrivial
two-sided straight segment through every point in a fixed normalized affine
chart would suffice, since squared Euclidean norm cannot attain a maximum
on a compact component containing such a segment through a maximizer.
No universal availability of these segments is assumed or proved here.
