## Original theorem and dependencies

For every realizable uniform rank-four parent M on eight labels, every
normalized parent component, and every internal antichain S of three valid
proper extension-feasibility regions, the original target is
`H_6(F_S; Q)=0`. The family is not restricted to the globally minimal
antichain E(M). Equal or dominated regions cannot supply a weakened target.

Write X for the normalized parent nine-manifold and B_i=X\F_i. The
supported-duality exact segment is

`H_7(X) -> H_c^2(union B_i) -> H_6(F_S) -> H_6(X)`.

Both outside groups vanish by the inherited contraction-height theorem for
the eight-element parent: its height base has dimension six. Thus D3 needs
no extra use of the blanket contractibility quotation for the exceptional
58 parents. This is a dependency reduction, not a new theorem.

The inherited single-bad two-skeleton theorem supplies
`H_c^q(B_i;Q)=0` for q=0,1,2. The second-diagonal theorem supplies
`H_c^0(B_i intersection B_j;Q)=0`. Compact-support Mayer--Vietoris for the
three closed semialgebraic bad loci then has exactly two surviving
total-degree-two associated-graded pieces:

* `H_c^0(B_0 intersection B_1 intersection B_2;Q)` at (p,q)=(2,0).
* The kernel of the signed restriction map
  `D=(r_01,-r_02,r_12): direct_sum H_c^1(B_i intersection B_j;Q)
  -> H_c^1(B_0 intersection B_1 intersection B_2;Q)` at (1,1).

Neither piece has an available higher incoming or outgoing differential.
The triple piece's only possible incoming maps originate at (1,0) and
(0,1), already zero. The pair piece has zero incoming single-bad source and
lies too close to both column boundaries for any higher differential.
Consequently vanishing of BOTH is necessary and sufficient. A split as
rational vector spaces is possible but not canonically supplied.

proper descent therefore supplies zero new original proof credit. The
latest no-continuous-section example does not contradict these comparisons.

## Alternative third diagonals

No diagonal among 3--9 is implied by the source theorems. The ninth has
the most different final endpoint: a complete labeled master-chamber graph
would reduce the universal claim to exact graph/cut-SAT tests. However,
all-parent geometric chamber and wall coverage is absent. The 26-cell
one-dimensional graph and an exact path between selected points are
scoped evidence only. The graph language is complete for a supplied graph;
it does not certify that graph is the global parent topology.

The fourth diagonal's inherited ambient `H_5(X)=0` removes one dependency,
not `H_5(F_S)` for four extensions. Its fivefold circuit calculation and
remaining cohomology terms are unresolved. The single and double contraction
routes supply no further diagonal: the first has an excessive base
dimension and the second introduces nonconvex/nonproper lift issues.

Conclusion: continue the assigned original pair and triple routes. There
is no source-supported free promotion or demonstrably cheaper completed
third diagonal available in this snapshot.
