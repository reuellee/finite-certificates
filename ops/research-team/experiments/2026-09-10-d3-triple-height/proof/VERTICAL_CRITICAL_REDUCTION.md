# The remaining triple obstruction can be confined to vertical critical points

## Statement and exact scope

Let X be any normalized connected component of any realizable uniform rank4
parent on eight labeled elements, and let P,Q,R be any three distinct primitive
residual factors. Choose ordinary four-normal occurrences for them as justified
in `../referee/ANCHOR_COVERAGE_AUDIT.md`. Every primitive global kind
36,38,48,49,50,51 has such an occurrence. In particular, a three-normal
localization occurrence must first be replaced by an equivalent ordinary one.

Let Z=X intersect {P=Q=R=0}. The ordinary occurrences have unique projective
concurrences p,q,r. Use the p-contraction coordinates supplied by the cited
audit: four projected-parent coordinates A in an open subset of R4, and four
parent heights h in a full open convex parent residence C_A. The normalization
retains every allowed projected coincidence, every original parent component,
and all of the finitely many clopen sign sectors.

On this chart let F(A,h),G(A,h) denote the Q and R occurrence determinants.
They differ from the primitive factors by nowhere-zero parent units, so their
vertical differential rank on Z is independent of that choice. Define

    K={Y in Z: rank(d_h F,d_h G)<2}.

Then K is closed in Z, every component of Z minus K is noncompact, and

    H_c^0(K;Q)=0  implies  H_c^0(Z;Q)=0.

This is a global reduction, not a proof that K has vanishing H_c^0. In
particular, rank conditions on a generic height fiber do not settle K.

There is a sharper version. Let Delta be the union of the three closed
concurrence-collision loci p=q, p=r and q=r. The accepted positive-height-fiber
argument, re-anchored at the repeated concurrence for each piece, gives
H_c^0(Delta)=0. Consequently it is enough to prove

    H_c^0(K minus Delta)=0.

Here K minus Delta is closed relative to Z minus Delta; it is not asserted
to be closed in Z. First apply localization to the closed subset Delta of Z,
then apply the regular/critical localization inside Z minus Delta. Removing
Delta does not destroy the regular-locus submersion, since its complement is
open. This order avoids assuming that an arbitrary intersection K intersect
Delta itself has vanishing H_c^0.

For clarity, the collision argument for p=q fixes their common contraction.
The Q block then imposes conditions only on projected parents. If r differs
from p, retaining its projected direction and one private height gives at
most four affine equations in the four parent heights and one private
height, with nonempty full convex fibers of dimension at least1. If r=p as
well, all four parent heights remain free. The same argument with another
anchor treats p=r and q=r. Each closed collision piece has H_c^0=0, including
its all-three-equal subpiece by closed/open localization. H_c^0 of their
finite closed union injects into the direct sum of their H_c^0 groups by
restriction, so the union vanishes too.

## Proof of the regular-locus assertion

The complete P wall in a normalization sector is an open strict-inequality
residence in A4 times R4. At a point of Z minus K, the two vertical derivatives
are independent. The implicit function theorem therefore makes projection of
Z minus K to A a submersion. This conclusion does not require either individual
height polynomial to be linear, nor a fixed choice of a nonzero minor on the
whole locus.

A semialgebraic connected component C of Z minus K is open in Z minus K. Its
projection is consequently a nonempty open subset of the open A4 base. If C
were compact, its projected image would be compact as well. A nonempty subset
of R4 cannot be both open and compact. Thus C is not compact.

The rank condition defining K is closed. For a locally compact semialgebraic
space, H_c^0 is the vector space generated by compact connected components.
Hence H_c^0(Z minus K)=0. The closed/open compact-support localization sequence
now gives the stated implication from H_c^0(K)=0. The argument applies on the
finite clopen normalization sectors and thus applies to the full original X.

## Pole-free circuit formula for the vertical gradients

This formula gives a direct exact discriminator for K without differentiating
a relabeled primitive polynomial or reconstructing a rational chart.

Work first with all eight heights before the three shear and one scale gauges.
For the fixed projection from p, write Y_j=z_j+h_j p using any chosen lift of
the projected column. For a Q triple I=(i1,i2,i3), put

    n_I(v)=det(Y_i1,Y_i2,Y_i3,v).

On the ordinary Q wall its four normal rows have rank3. Let lambda_I be a
nonzero dependence among them and choose a nonzero representative q of their
common kernel. Every lambda_I is nonzero by the ordinary-circuit source
theorem. Define the eight-vector

    gamma_Q,j = sum over I containing j of
                lambda_I det(Y_i1,...,p in the j slot,...,Y_i3,q).

Define gamma_R in exactly the same way, using its dependence and concurrence.
The actual differential of the four-normal determinant in the eight heights
is a nonzero scalar multiple of gamma_Q. Indeed the derivative of a rank3
determinant is its rank1 adjugate pairing, and the adjugate is a nonzero scalar
multiple of q times lambda-transpose. Differentiating each normal in the
height direction replaces the indicated parent column by p, which is precisely
the displayed formula.

Both gamma vectors annihilate the three height-shear directions and the
actual height-scaling direction. These transformations are projective changes
of the parent that preserve each wall. The four gauge directions are
independent at every uniform point because the selected high-parent bracket
is nonzero. Therefore they induce precisely the two vertical cotangent
vectors on the four-dimensional normalized height space. In particular,

    Y is in K iff gamma_Q and gamma_R are linearly dependent in R8.

Changing representatives of p,q,r or either circuit dependence scales the
vectors by nonzero scalars. Multiplying a wall equation by a parent unit does
the same on its zero set. Thus this rank test retains its meaning across
every allowed sign sector and avoids artificial poles.

## The known simultaneous critical canary is a concurrence collision

The exact source point for canonical factors (39,48,50) is

    (a,b,c,d,e,f,g,h,i)=(-1,2,3,4,-5,2,7,-4,5),

in the original normalization with columns6=(1,a,b,c),7=(1,d,e,f),
8=(1,g,h,i). All70 parent brackets are nonzero. The ordinary occurrences

    P48=123/145/246/356,
    Q39=125/126/356/378,
    R50=123/145/246/378

have concurrence points

    p=(1/2,1,1,0), q=(1/2,1,0,0), r=(1/2,1,1,0).

Thus p=r, and both vertical gradient vectors vanish. This explains why this
known failure of global gradient independence does not challenge the
already-established positive-fiber treatment of concurrence collisions.
There is also the exact global polynomial identity

    q39=(f-i) q48+(1-c) q50.

Consequently this entire triple zero set is the already-proved pair zero set
{q48=q50=0}; its known noncompactness is not a new triple closure.

The Q39 replacement has a direct incidence proof. Its localization asks the
planes123,356,378 to share a line through parent3. Since 356 and378 are
distinct, their intersection is a line through3. That line lies in123 exactly
when it meets line12. Equivalently, the four planes125,126,356,378 have a common
point: 125 intersect126 is line12 by parent uniformity. The common point is
unique and none of the ordinary triple-normal minors vanishes on this wall.
The last ordinary-circuit assertion can alternatively be read from its type37
relabeling: (1,2,3,4,5,6,7,8) maps to (1,2,5,6,3,7,8,4).

`critical_canary.py` reconstructs the parent brackets, ordinary normals,
concurrences, circuit dependencies and vertical-gradient rank exactly. This
is a replay of one source canary, not a new triple-orbit closure.

## Remaining discriminator

After removing all three closed pairwise-collision pieces, determine whether an actual
uniform point in K can have three noncollinear concurrence points. A proposed
implication from vertical criticality to collinearity is presently unproved.
Even if established, a separate argument must handle distinct collinear
points: equal projected directions alone does not, by dimension count, force
the eight-by-six height coefficient matrix to have rank at most5.

An actual noncollinear critical point would refute that proposed localization,
but would not refute global triple noncompactness. A global proof of
H_c^0(K)=0, or of noncompactness for its full closed critical pieces, would
complete the missing all-factor triple endpoint when combined with the
accepted pair endpoint. No original diagonal is claimed here.
