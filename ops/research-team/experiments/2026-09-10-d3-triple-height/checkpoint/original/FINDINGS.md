## Why the remaining conic route needs more

We derived the full symbolic conic for the populated balanced type50+50
canary while changing the retained coordinate c. Its first-seven-parent
chirotope is constant throughout -8<c<-3. The conic determinant has a simple
factor c+4 in that interval, and several pulled-back parent-bracket
discriminants vanish at other interior parameter values. Thus neither a
uniform nondegenerate-conic assertion nor an unchanged end-incidence pattern
can be assumed over the deletion base.

The symbolic discovery is recorded in CONIC_SPECIALIZATION_DISCOVERY.json
and probe_conic_specialization.py. It is exploratory and does not carry the
529-case theorem or any global topological claim. The independent falsifier
computed actual one/two/one conic-fiber component counts in the anchor parent
sign chamber; their exact artifact and scope belong to the falsifier track.

In general even a smooth projection whose fibers are unions of open
intervals can have a nonzero compactly supported section of R^1 f_!. For
example R^2 minus the origin projects to R with one interval off zero and
two intervals at zero; the difference at zero is a compactly supported
degree-one class. The affine theorem avoids this problem by proving that
the full rank-two fiber is a single convex interval and handling coefficient
rank drop separately. A generic conic theorem still lacks that property.
