## Required audit for a positive selection/continuation claim

The normalized witness polytope is
`P(Y) = { w >= 0 : 1^T w = 1, A(Y)^T w = 0 }` in the fixed literal gauge.
It is compact and convex. On a nonempty fiber the Euclidean minimizer exists
uniquely. This alone proves no continuity in Y: feasible sets can fail lower
semicontinuity when rank/support degenerates.

A claimed sufficient condition must explicitly supply the local feasible
approximants needed for continuity. A standard usable condition is a strictly
positive normalized witness on a support S for which the 5-by-|S| augmented
matrix `[A_S^T;1^T]` has row rank five. A continuous right inverse then corrects
small residuals using S. Mixing with a persisting positive reserve witness
can make this correction nonnegative for any fixed starting witness. Compactness
and uniqueness then imply continuity of the minimum-norm selector. This
condition is local, sufficient, and stronger than mere badness; it cannot be
assumed at all points of the full bad locus.

For a concrete interval certificate, independently check:

1. Literal polynomial/rational parent family and interval, with all 70 signed
   brackets nonzero throughout, not just at sample points.
2. The complete raw normal/sign convention and the intended fixed signature.
3. Full rational-function identities `A(t)^T w(t)=0`, `1^T w(t)=1`.
4. Nonnegativity throughout the closed interval, including one-sided joins,
   denominator zeros, endpoint specializations and support changes.
5. Rank claims from exact minors and cofactor identities. Every claimed
   all-parameter sign uses a complete interval argument, such as Bernstein
   positivity or root isolation, not an unproved grid extrapolation.
6. Continuity at joins, equality to the required starting witness, and any
   distinction between a continuous witness and the minimum-norm witness.
7. If optimum claims are made, rational KKT or equivalent certificates covering
   all 56 coordinates; optimum on one chosen support is not global optimum.
8. Hostile controls changing a weight, parent parameter, signature bit, support,
   interval endpoint, claimed polynomial coefficient and continuity join.
