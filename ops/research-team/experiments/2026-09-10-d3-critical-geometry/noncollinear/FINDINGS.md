concurrence points do not force independent vertical gradients. An exact
actual witness in global kinds48,36,49 has concurrence span rank3 and height
gradient rank1. Both independent falsifier and referee reconstructions accept
it. This is a negative result about the proposed classification, not about
triple noncompactness or original injectivity.

The witness is therefore not a surviving compact triple component.
