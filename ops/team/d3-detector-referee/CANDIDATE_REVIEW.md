# Independent review of boundary connecting-map detectors

This is an independent deductive audit of the candidate communicated by the
constructive prover. It uses compact-support localization and the two-set
closed-cover sequence directly; no producer acceptance routine is used.
Coefficient field is Q. All statements concern the actual bad loci when the
inherited single-bad vanishings are applicable.

## The first proposed reverse arrow is unavailable

For distinct i,j,k, let `Uij=Bi\Bj` and `Eik=Aik\Bj`. The localization
sequence for `Aij⊂Bi`, together with `Hc1(Bi)=Hc2(Bi)=0`, gives an isomorphism
`θij:Hc1(Aij)→Hc2(Uij)`. Restriction along `Aik⊂Bi` gives the naturality
identity

`δik rij = q θij`,

where `q:Hc2(Uij)→Hc2(Eik)` is restriction and `δik` is the frontier map for
`T⊂Aik`. The subset Eik is **closed** in Uij. Extension by zero for an open
inclusion therefore does not supply a reverse arrow to q.

Writing `Ci=Bi\(Bj∪Bk)`, the exact obstruction sequence is

`Hc2(Ci)→Hc2(Uij) --q→ Hc2(Eik)→Hc3(Ci)`.

This describes lift existence and ambiguity. A chosen rational section on
an image still does not establish selective identities against the two rival
pair colors. In particular, mere surjectivity of q is not a detector theorem.

## The refined candidate is a genuine canonical map

Set `Wi=Aij∪Aik`, a closed subset of Bi, and retain the open complement Ci.
The closed-cover sequence for Wi has the segment

`Hc1(Aij)⊕Hc1(Aik)→Hc1(T) --μi→ Hc2(Wi)`.

The first arrow is a difference of restrictions after ordering the cover;
its image is the sum `im rij+im rik`, independently of that ordering.
Consequently `ker μi=im rij+im rik`.

The localization sequence for `Wi⊂Bi` has

`Hc2(Bi)→Hc2(Wi) --δi→ Hc3(Ci)`.

Since `Hc2(Bi)=0`, δi is injective. The composite

`τi=δi μi : Hc1(T)→Hc3(Ci)`

is therefore canonical up to the conventional overall cover sign, with

`ker τi = im rij+im rik`.

It kills both incident pair images without requiring an illegitimate
closed-extension map. Define `κi=τi rjk` on the opposite pair group.

## Nondegeneracy is equivalent to the original target

Suppose all κi are injective. If `r01 x01-r02 x02+r12 x12=0`, applying τ0
gives `κ0 x12=0`, so x12=0. Applying τ1 gives `-κ1 x02=0`, so x02=0.
Applying τ2 gives `κ2 x01=0`, so x01=0. Hence D is injective.

Conversely, suppose D is injective and `κi xjk=0`. The kernel formula says
`rjk xjk` is a sum of the two incident pair images. Moving those terms to
one side and assigning the fixed signs `(1,-1,1)` gives a D-kernel element
whose opposite component is xjk, up to sign. Injectivity implies xjk=0.
Thus every κi is injective. This argument requires no `Hc0(T)=0` hypothesis.

This is a valid construction of actual-space connecting maps. The three
unproved nondegeneracy assertions are together **exactly equivalent** to
original D injectivity. They are not smaller global obligations. Single-bad
vanishing does not determine `Hc3(Ci)` or the maps κi, since the two good-block
conditions defining Ci can truncate the original residence fibers and alter
their ends. The surviving input is a quantified geometric separation theorem
for these actual exclusive-singleton boundary classes, or the original
compared signed factor-end data.
