proper. For a finite family of already declared semialgebraic subsets/maps,

## Required distinctions

### Parent matrices versus witness/support rank

A parent-matrix rank drop changes the actual realization matrix and may land
on nonuniform parent boundary when the compactification attachment is proved.
A witness augmented-kernel rank drop changes the dimension/support of a
Gordan face over a still-uniform parent. Occurrence rank, concurrence rank,
support loss and witness-kernel rank are therefore internal theorem strata
unless a separate source-derived map proves otherwise. The single-bad
coordinate-face filtration correctly treats witness support/rank as internal;
it does not globalize this distinction to the three-block joined complex.

### Local cutoff versus true parent infinity

True infinity is `I=Xbar\X` for the actual parent compactification. A chart
divisor covered by another chart, a local rational box face, a cube boundary,
a collar/work-scope edge, zero block mass, or a witness degeneration is not in
`I`. The full-space local roadmap only proves contact with artificial box
faces. The hybrid-cube and row-2599 local results likewise require separate
parent-boundary attachment before they can count for C.

### Per-block partitions versus a simultaneous subdivision

Separate coordinate-face filtrations or per-block Hardt partitions do not
produce the required joined complex. A and B require one source-derived
refinement simultaneously compatible with all three labeled blocks, every
`1<=r<=3` active subset, every labeled placement modulo permutation, all
supports, all zero-weight faces, charts, residual walls, rank strata and the
parent boundary. No frozen source constructs this common refinement.

### Codimension-one seams versus arbitrary face-chain coherence

A wall cospan or a collection of commuting squares does not define a functor
on the full face category. Iterated support loss, zero weights, chart changes
and boundary specialization can be composed in different orders. A/B need
equal induced chain maps (including labels and orientations) for every
composable flag and coherent monodromy around loops. The frozen sources give
local cospans and examples but no full face category, comparison natural
transformation, or higher coherence theorem.
