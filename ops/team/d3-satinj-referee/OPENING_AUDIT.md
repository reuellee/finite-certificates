## Minimum mathematical acceptance criteria

1. **An actual scoped lemma.** Identify the original normalized parent domain,
   its proper pairwise incomparable extension labels, the actual bad/pair/
   triple/exclusive loci, and the claimed comparison or attachment map. A
   chosen normal slice is insufficient unless a proved global comparison
   supplies the missing compact-support degree shifts and boundary maps.
2. **A failure implication.** Every failure of the stated lemma inside its
   domain must yield a point of the nominated polynomial system. A rank-drop
   locus alone is not a failure locus for an arbitrary topology or injectivity
   statement. The required implication must be proved before an emptiness
   calculation can certify the lemma.
3. **Exact localization and branch accounting.** Every inverted factor must
   be proved nonzero throughout the claimed domain, or its vanishing branch
   must remain as a separately attached obligation. Rank drops, zero witness
   weights, residual factors and chart seams cannot be discarded as if they
   were true parent infinity. Parameter parent coordinates remain variable.
4. **Real-domain accounting.** Complex algebraic exclusion on a localization
   is sufficient for real exclusion there, but surviving complex solutions
   need not be real failures. Strict signs, normalized witness nonnegativity,
   the other blocks' good-locus conditions and actual closure must be retained
   in any claimed real geometric interpretation. Weak inequalities do not
   automatically give the closure of a strict-sign locus.
5. **Proof-producing arithmetic.** For a claimed empty localization, record
   the exact source equations, coefficient field, saturation factors and an
   independently checkable rational polynomial identity (or another exact
   certificate of the same conclusion). Modular agreement alone is discovery
   evidence. A characteristic-specific certificate is not a rational proof.
6. **The attachment conclusion.** Prove that the geometric fact determines
   an actual signed frontier/comparison reduction for the original map D,
   including overlap compatibility, orientation, zero-weight specializations
   and true-infinity attachment. Nonsingularity does not imply properness,
   trivial monodromy, global triviality, or cohomological injectivity.
7. **Quantified coverage.** State exactly what original obligation closes,
   or give a strictly smaller certified exhaustive residual with complete
   attachment. One successful local system, a list of possible equations, or
   a revised abstract kernel identity earns no theorem-distance decrease.

These criteria are acceptance requirements, not a producer design. The
