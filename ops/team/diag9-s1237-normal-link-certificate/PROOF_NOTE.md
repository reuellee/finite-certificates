## Result

The fail-closed certificate envelope and replay boundary for
`D9_S1237_4SUPPORT_NORMAL_LINK_GATE1` are fixed, but no producer normal-link
payload was present on this isolated opening head.  No mathematical endpoint
is therefore asserted.

Version 1 accepts only exact unresolved or timeout frontiers.  It parses and
preflights the complete/no-go artifact shapes, source compositions, rational
sector witnesses, and domination inequalities, but refuses to return a
mathematical endpoint until the producer supplies its actual partition and
obstruction witness methods and an independent adapter is versioned for those
methods.  This prevents a plausible-looking digest from standing in for
geometry that does not yet exist.

The verifier independently reconstructs the complete S12,37 active-literal
census from pinned determinant and circuit sources.  It retains all global
occurrences of each of the 3,539 active factor classes, the lexically first
representative, primitive polynomial, stripped parent-unit bracket labels,
fixed unit sign to the representative, family signature incidence, and both
raw and representative allowed orientations.  The 5,026 aligned occurrence
union is checked independently.  The canonical census semantic digest is
`a1b9d3d9da1e01df83621dc8f1c7959f86ae2e0d9bd3bc457124c561cbac245a`.

The endpoint-neutral envelope represents complete, no-go, unresolved, and
timeout outcomes.  A complete outcome requires all 63 nonempty normal-axis
supports for each face, recursive facet/base/apex/seam/coface closure, every
active literal and parent bracket normal form on every stratum, exact sector
feasibility or Gordan infeasibility witnesses, and exact higher-order
domination.  A no-go outcome may stop at the first exact obstruction.  Null
and timeout outcomes must give a disjoint processed/pending dependency
frontier and deterministic resume command.

The shipped JSON is intentionally marked sample-only and has no artifacts.
Default replay proves that it is rejected; it must not be mistaken for an
unresolved mathematical result.  The self-test then rejects eleven hostile

stabilization, collar/topology, and ledger scope.

## Nonconsequences

This engineering result is not a normal-link computation.  It proves no
collar, does not glue through compactification boundary faces, supplies no
global active-sector coverage or separator, and does not decide diagonal
nine.  The theorem ledger remains `2/9`.
