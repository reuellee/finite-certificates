## Selected experiment contract

### Question

Can a deterministic exact generator and independent verifier construct the
order-two closure data for the strict row-2599 parent interior while treating
every genuine parent-wall escape as relative?

### Version-one certificate interface

A generated certificate must contain:

1. a pinned active-factor input and exact chart/domain identifiers;
2. nonrelative cell IDs, dimensions, exact witnesses and factor signs;
3. every strict closure-comparable pair and strict three-cell chain needed by
   the barycentric two-skeleton;
4. simultaneous-factor and rank-drop tags;
5. genuine parent-infinity/frontier tags, with no local-scope boundary
   substitution;
6. complete bad-signature labels whose lower-cell sets are closed;
7. coverage, disjointness, no-duplicate and regular-ball certificates;
8. integral signed incidences satisfying `d^2=0`;
9. the unsigned mod-two middle-rank replay; and
10. deterministic build metadata and an independent verifier.

### Canaries

The verifier must reject:

- an unsigned moving-column transport that flips a target-signed parent
  factor;
- a sampled chart bank presented as parameter-space coverage;
- a local outer boundary mislabeled as parent infinity;
- a closure relation that does not decrease dimension;
- a corrupted empty-factor or residue digest.

### Stop rule

Every projection, CAD, or roadmap pilot must declare one finite layer and
preserve its exact frontier if it exceeds the resource ceiling. A failed pilot
must identify the generator obstruction it exposes. It may not be followed
automatically by a wider version of the same search.

## Tooling consequence

A complete sign-invariant CAD is not automatically the right object. The pair
proof only needs connected strata and order-two closure incidence, so exact
semialgebraic roadmap or critical-point methods should be tested as compiler
backends. SAT/SMT, modular arithmetic, numerical optimization, and CAS may
discover decompositions or identities, but exact coverage and independent
replay remain the trust boundary.

A proof assistant is a sensible later target for the small stable reductions:
the obligation split and the integral-lift/mod-two rank implication. It will
not replace the generated coverage certificate.
