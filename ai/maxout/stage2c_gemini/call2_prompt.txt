THE VERIFICATION PRINCIPLE (the key methodological upgrade — implement it exactly):
The signed 3x3 determinants p_abc = det(u_a,u_b,u_c) of the 5 direction vectors in R^3 are the Pluecker coordinates of a point of Gr(3,5). The ideal of ALL polynomial relations among the ten p_abc is generated by the 5 classical three-term Grassmann-Pluecker relations (for each index a in {0..4} with the other four indices i1<i2<i3<i4: p_{a,i1,i2} p_{a,i3,i4} - p_{a,i1,i3} p_{a,i2,i4} + p_{a,i1,i4} p_{a,i2,i3} = 0). Per fixed chirotope, D_abc = chi_abc * p_abc with known signs chi_abc; compute chi exactly from the integer U_ints in reference_structure.json and record it.

A candidate symbolic certificate y(D) (entries polynomial in the D's after clearing denominators — see below) for system (sigma, k) is verified RIGOROUSLY CELL-WIDE by:
(a) EQUALITY, weight columns: for each t in 0..4, the w_t-column identity of B^T y = 0 — sum over support side rows of y_row * (its signed s_t D_tij entry) plus the weight-row multiplier when w_t is in the support — must reduce to 0 modulo the GP ideal (substitute D_abc = chi_abc p_abc, reduce over QQ[p]).
(b) EQUALITY, T columns: the vector identity sum over support side rows of y_row * (+/- C_ij) = 0, where C_ij = u_i x u_j. Since <u_t, u_i x u_j> = det(u_t,u_i,u_j) = p_tij (signed!), dot the vector identity with each u_t, t=0..4: five scalar polynomial identities in the p's; each must reduce to 0 modulo the GP ideal. (The five u_t span R^3, so the five scalar identities are equivalent to the vector identity.)
Use sympy groebner over QQ in the ten p variables (grevlex; degrees are low), or explicit reduction with the 5 GP relations as rewrite rules — either way EXACT.
(c) POSITIVITY: after clearing denominators (multiply the whole certificate by the positive common denominator — a monomial in the D's, strictly positive on the open cell — this preserves both equalities and positivity), each multiplier is a polynomial in the D's. Classify each as one of:
  (i)  PROVEN POSITIVE: coefficientwise nonnegative in the D's and not identically zero — possibly after adding an element of the GP ideal (search low-degree GP-multiples to rewrite into a manifestly nonnegative form);
  (ii) NOT CELL-WIDE: exhibit an exact rational configuration IN THE SAME chirotope cell (verify the full chirotope equality exactly) where the multiplier evaluates negative. Cheap generator of such probes: rescale a single u_i by a positive rational (preserves the chirotope, changes D ratios), plus small exact rational perturbations of U_ints re-checked for chirotope equality;
  (iii) UNDECIDED: neither found within budget.
A certificate is PROVEN CELL-WIDE only if (a)+(b) hold as ideal membership AND every multiplier is class (i). If (a)+(b) hold but some multiplier is class (ii), record honestly as SUBCELL-ONLY (valid on the subcell where those multipliers are nonnegative) — that is NOT a cell-wide certificate. Class (iii) anywhere -> CONJECTURED at best.
