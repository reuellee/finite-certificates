## Plücker Ideal Verification and the Sign Bug

To verify a Gordan certificate $B^T y = 0, y \ge 0, y \neq 0$ cell-wide, we parameterize the entries of $y$ as polynomials in the positive Plücker absolute determinants $D_{ijk}$. The vector $B^T y$ consists of 8 entries corresponding to the primal variables: 3 directional $T$ coordinates and 5 weight $w$ coordinates. We substitute $D_{ijk}$ into the exact signed Plücker variables $p_{ijk}$ and require the resulting polynomials to be exactly zero modulo the Grassmann-Plücker ideal (generated by the 5 three-term algebraic identities on 5 points). 

**The Bug:** The verifier `check_cellwide.py` (written in Call 2) correctly built the $T$ equations. However, for the weight equations $W_t$, the true Gordan condition demands $\sum y_i B_{i, w_t} + y_{w_t} = 0$. Since $B_{i, w_t}$ entries could be positive, the only way a strictly positive $y$ can satisfy this is if the side contributions $\sum y_i B_{i, w_t}$ are negative. The verifier erroneously formulated the check as `W_eqs[t] -= y_s` instead of `W_eqs[t] += y_s`, effectively checking $\sum y_i B_{i, w_t} - y_{w_t} = 0$. 

This caused `check_cellwide.py` to erroneously accept solutions that actually proved $\sum y_i B_{i, w_t} > 0$ (the exact opposite of the zero condition required by Gordan's theorem). Because of this bug, Call 2 proudly claimed cell-wide certificates for targets like `32316` and `55859`.

## Phase B: The Single-Class Systematic Family

Fixing the verifier sign bug reveals the *true* single-class equal-pair family.
For a specific class $(i, j)$ and side split $s \in \{\pm 1\}^5$, an equal-pair certificate $y_{(i,j,+)} = y_{(i,j,-)} = y_{pair}$ cancels the $T$ variables exactly. The remaining weight conditions require the side contributions to be strictly negative to balance $y_{w_t} > 0$.

The exact criterion for a single-class certificate to exist is:
$$ \sigma_{ij,+} = \sigma_{ij,-} \quad \text{AND} \quad \sigma_{ij} s_t = -1 \quad \text{for all } t \notin \{i,j\} $$

Under this combinatorial criterion, the weight coordinate conditions become $-y_{pair} D_{tij} + y_{w_t} = 0$, giving the strictly positive coefficient formula:
$$ y_{(i,j,+)} = y_{(i,j,-)} = D_{t_1 t_2 t_3} $$
$$ y_{w_t} = D_{t_1 t_2 t_3} D_{tij} $$

**Scaling the scan across all 33,140 x 2 configurations gives:**
- **33,437** systems are covered by this single-class family (roughly 50% of the total 66,280 space).
- **0** overlap with the 100 failed Stage 2c targets (symbolic_gp_results.json).
- **0** overlap with the both-splits residue from Stage 2b-1.
- **30,650** overlap with the Stage 2b-1 equal-pair covered set.

The family works perfectly, but entirely outside the difficult residue regions, confirming that equal-pair algebraic matching cannot penetrate the residue.
