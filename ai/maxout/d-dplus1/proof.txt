The Maximum Number of Vertices of a
(d, d + 1)-Zonoboxtope Is 2d+2− 6
Reuel Lee
August 2, 2026
Abstract
A (d,n )-zonoboxtope is the convex hull of two weighted Minkowski sums of a common family
of n line segments inRd. We determine its maximal vertex number whenn =d + 1. For every
d≥ 2, the maximum is
2d+2− 6.
The proof reduces the separating-hypersurface test to comparisons between two lists ofd + 1
real numbers. Their global maximum and global minimum force two distinct chambers to
be monocolored, giving the upper bound. An explicit rational family has exactly those two
monocolored chambers and attains the bound. Consequently, the(4, 5) maximum is 58, and
Part 2 of Conjecture 6.6 in Balakin–Cox–Loho–Sturmfels is false for everyd≥ 4 at n =d + 1.

3 Two chambers must be monocolored
Lemma 2. Assumen≥ 3 and the 2n numbersp1,...,p n,q 1,...,q n are distinct. Then at least two
distinct chambers CP are monocolored.
Proof. Consider the global maximum among the2n numbers. If it ispr, then every valuepr−qj
with j̸= r is positive, soC{r} is monocolored. If it isqr, then every valuepi−qr with i̸= r is
negative, soC[n]\{r} is monocolored.
The global minimum gives another monocolored chamber by the same argument with signs
reversed: ps givesC{s}, whileqs givesC[n]\{s}. These two chambers are distinct. For extrema of the
same type this follows from distinctness; for extrema of opposite types it follows because a singleton
cannot equal the complement of a singleton whenn≥ 3.
Combining Lemma 2 with (10) gives
f0(Q)≤R + (R− 2) = 2R− 2 = 2 d+2− 6.
The perturbation observation at the start of Section 2 extends this bound to all, possibly nongeneric,
instances.

5 Consequences and verification
Corollary 3. The maximal number of vertices of a (4, 5)-zonoboxtope is 58.
Corollary 4. Part 2 of Conjecture 6.6 in [ 1] is false for every d≥ 4: at n = d + 1 it predicts
2d+2− 4, while the exact value is 2d+2− 6.
The accompanying script verify_d_dplus1.py uses only the Python standard library. For
any requested finite range of dimensions it checks the rational realization, the unique positive
circuit, all chamber signs, the two exceptional subsets, and the resulting vertex count. The script is
supplementary: the proof above is symbolic and applies to everyd≥ 2.
